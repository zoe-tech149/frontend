Case Summary

Between 11:30 p.m. and 5:45 a.m., an unknown person broke into Rosie's Bakery and stole the cash box, a commercial mixer and a tray of wedding cakes.

Status: Under investigation

Incident Details

Reported on 7 November 2026 by the owner, Rosie Patel.
Point of entry: rear window, size 0.5 m×0.5 m 0.8 m×0.6 m
Time of break-in: 2:00 a.m. 3:15 a.m. (confirmed by CCTV)
The back door was unlocked from the inside.
Total estimated loss: R 18,500.

Witness Statement

I saw a tall person carrying a large box at about three in the morning. They were whistling and got into a white van with no lights on.

— Mrs. Hattie Oakes, neighbour, 14 Sprinkle Street

Suspect Interview

Person of interest: Benny Cole, former delivery driver.
When asked where he was that night he said, I was at home, asleep.
Alibi: working a night shift — checked and ruled out, no shift was logged.

Forensic Report

Fingerprints on the window frame were developed with ninhydrin (C9H6O4). Prints were found on the 2nd pane from the left.
Footprint cast: size 10 boot, pressed 3 cm into soft soil.
Cake tray volume: 60 cm × 40 cm × 8 cm = 19,200 cm3.
DNA swabs were taken from the broken glass.

The thief’s modus operandi (method of working) matches two other break-ins in the district.
